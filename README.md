# Zopshare for Windows

**Share to any device.** Send files between your PC, Android phones and other PCs on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.

✅ Works offline: no internet needed
✅ Uses no mobile data
✅ No cables, no account, no sign-up
✅ Files go straight from one device to the other and are never uploaded anywhere

---

## Download

1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases/latest) page.
2. Download **`Zopshare.exe`**.
3. Double-click it to run. Nothing needs to be installed.

> **"Windows protected your PC" message?**
> This is normal for new apps that aren't widely known yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**For your phone:** install **Zopshare: Share to Any Device** from the Google Play Store.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- The PC and the other device must be on the **same Wi-Fi network**, or the PC must be connected to the **phone's hotspot**

No Wi-Fi router? Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it. Files are still sent directly between the two devices, so this uses **no mobile data**.

---

## First-time setup

1. **Allow Zopshare through the firewall.** The first time you receive files, Windows asks whether to allow Zopshare on your network. Tick **Private networks** and click **Allow**.
2. **Set your Wi-Fi to Private.** Open **Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi**, click your network, and choose **Private network**. On a Public network, Windows hides your PC from other devices. Zopshare shows a warning if this is the problem.

## How to use

### Send files from your PC
1. Open Zopshare.
2. Drag files onto the window, or click **choose files**.
3. Pick the phone or PC from the list and click **Send**.

### Receive files on your PC
1. Click **Start receiving**.
2. On the phone, open Zopshare and pick your PC's name.

Received files are saved in your **Downloads** folder. Click **Open** or **Show in folder** to find them. You can change the save folder in **Settings**.

## Common questions

**Does it use the internet or my mobile data?**
No. Files travel directly between your devices over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot. Zopshare works with no internet connection at all.

**My PC doesn't show up on the phone. What should I do?**
- Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Check that your Wi-Fi is set to **Private** (see First-time setup).
- Make sure you clicked **Start receiving** on the PC.
- Still not working? Zopshare shows your PC's IP address. On the phone, tap **Having trouble?** and type that address.

**Are my files safe?**
Your files are never uploaded to any server. They go straight from one device to the other on your own network.

**Is there a size limit?**
No. You can send large files, such as videos, and many files at once.

**Can I share between two PCs?**
Yes. Run Zopshare on both PCs.
